# 03 · Facts register

**The only numbers and claims anyone may publish.** Every claim in public content must trace to an id here.

Status: **ready** = publish with its source · **framed** = publish only as described · **needs-source** = don't publish until the source is filed · **conflict** = the team must decide.

| Id | Claim | Source | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `example.customers` | 1,000+ customers | CRM export, 1 Nov | needs-source | Replace with your own |

## How to add a fact

1. Open a pull request that adds a row with `needs-source`.
2. Attach the source (link, or a path in your shared drive).
3. The brand owner changes the status to `ready` when merging.
